# 供应链控制塔路线图（2026-09-04）

来源：三份审计（docs/engineering/审计-*-2026-09-04.md）、NOW.md、CURRENT.md D50–D68。可分享页：见会话 artifact「供应链控制塔路线图」。


## 1 · Workflows 工作流：预警 → 待办 → 决策 → 结果

- **W1 Move the six older watchdogs onto the alert engine — doc-aging, data-freshness, job-failure, data-product-gate, jst-token, weekly-dq** · 证据：They still insert alerts by hand with no owner role, action link, dedupe key, or event history; notifications route them by a hard-coded category table. · 收益：One alert contract: correct routing, close reasons, precision tracking, and the same acknowledge/close buttons for every category. · 工作量 M · 依赖：engineering
- **W2 Manual close with reason in the alert pages — AlertCloseModal exists, not yet mounted** · 证据：Close route and reason codes shipped; the UI still only offers 已知悉. Resolved view does not show why an alert was closed. · 收益：People can close a false positive with a reason; the reason feeds precision and threshold tuning. · 工作量 S · 依赖：engineering
- **W3 Explain every replenishment row — targetBasis, safetyDaysBasis, noSuggestReason** · 证据：Target cover days come from a four-layer parameter resolver but the row does not say which layer matched; an empty 建议 cell cannot be told apart from "engine could not compute". · 收益：Planners see "target 45d from brand rule" and "no suggestion: ref-gap suppressed" instead of guessing. · 工作量 S · 依赖：engineering
- **W4 Expiry-aware transfer suggestions — FEFO donor selection; expired batches never move** · 证据：transfer-suggest balances quantities only; it can move fresh stock while 90-day batches sit in the donor warehouse. · 收益：Transfers become a write-off avoidance tool, not just a balancing tool. · 工作量 M · 依赖：engineering
- **W5 Decline state returned by the API + suppression review — did a stockout follow a held suggestion?** · 证据：「不采纳」 is stored in the audit trail but the row only shows it in the same browser; the ref-gap gate withheld 115k units with no review of whether that was right. · 收益：Decline visible to everyone; a monthly answer to "was the suppression correct". · 工作量 M · 依赖：engineering
- **W6 Todo deadline from the real order-by date — timePhasedNetReq instead of the on-hand approximation** · 证据：Due date is now derived from cover minus lead time; the replenishment engine already computes the exact latest order date. · 收益：Deadlines match what the planner sees on the replenish page. · 工作量 S · 依赖：engineering
- **W7 Learned lead time switches from observe-only to applied — P90 with sample gate, shown as its own basis segment** · 证据：rollup_supplier_lead is now shown beside the archive value. Applying it needs one observed cycle and real PO history, and the system has one PO this year. · 收益：Thresholds stop trusting hand-typed lead times for chronically late suppliers. · 工作量 S · 依赖：needs data business POs in system
- **W8 Threshold tuning proposals from alert precision — auto-propose alert_buffer_days changes with evidence; human approves** · 证据：The 05:30 verification job starts scoring closed stockout alerts as true/false positives from today; after 2–4 weeks there is enough to tune. · 收益：Fewer false alerts without anyone hand-editing parameters blind. · 工作量 M · 依赖：needs data 2–4 weeks of alert_events
- **W9 Exception snooze / dismiss with audit — plus "fired N days, never clicked" metric** · 证据：Exceptions on the workbench are recomputed each visit with no memory; nothing records that an item has been shown for 40 days and ignored. · 收益：Directors can park an item with a date and see chronic ignored exceptions. · 工作量 M · 依赖：engineering
- **W10 Goals linked to the alerts and todos that move them — forecast-to-period-end** · 证据：A goal at 60% on the 3rd reads the same as 60% on the 25th; goals have no owning work items. · 收益：Each goal shows its trajectory and the open items that would change it. · 工作量 M · 依赖：business set at least one goal first
- **W11 S&OP close-out and pilot A/B read-out — plan vs actual by tier and ownership class** · 证据：The S&OP cycle ends in a terminal state with no variance report; pilot SKUs are flagged but never compared against joint-review SKUs. · 收益：The cycle becomes a learning loop; the pilot answers whether supply-chain-direct decisions are better. · 工作量 L · 依赖：needs data several cycles
- **W12 Value-weighted tiering as a parallel column — sales6m × unit cost from core/valuation** · 证据：Tiers are by units, so a cheap bulk SKU outranks a strategic one. Production valuation coverage is 99.98% via the finance cost observation, so the switch is feasible. · 收益：A-class means "matters to the business"; every downstream rule inherits it after a comparison period. · 工作量 M · 依赖：engineering ship parallel, do not repoint consumers yet
- **W13 Feishu private-message delivery and per-role digest** · 证据：Reminders to individuals need a Feishu app bot; today only the group webhook works. · 收益：Deadline reminders and personal alerts reach people directly. · 工作量 S · 依赖：business create the Feishu app

## 6 · How we will know it worked 验收指标

- **Alert precision by rule**：true positives ÷ scored, from the verification job（target ≥ 70% for inventory_cover within 6 weeks）
- **Cockpit p95 latency**：measured in production at 11:05 and 17:05（target < 1.5 s warm, no inline rebuilds）
- **Todo on-time rate (strict)**：done before the real order-by date（tracked monthly per role, evidence not ranking）
- **Identity coverage**：mapped platform SKUs ÷ platform SKUs（from 23.8% to ≥ 70%）
- **Pilot candidates auto-direct**：S/A/B with known lead times and stable demand（from 0 to the 80/20 rule once lead times are entered）
- **Suggestion accuracy distribution**：ordered and realized vs suggested, by bucket（reported as a distribution with n, never a single score）
