## Part A — Area-by-area findings

### A1. Inventory cover / stockout alerts

**Files**: `src/server/rules/alert-threshold.ts`, `src/server/rules/alert-priority.ts`, `src/server/modules/report/inventory-alerts.ts`, `src/jobs/alert-watchdogs.ts`

**(1) Decision & inputs.** Per active finished SKU: `coverDays = onHand / primaryDaily` vs `alertDays = production + logistics + buffer`, producing `alert/watch/ok` and one primary alert kind. Inputs: `getOnHandBySku` (`src/server/core/stock-view.ts`), three daily-velocity candidates (external Tmall net30/30, `sales_monthly` 6-month via `dailyFromWindow`, `stock_ledger` out-30d/30), `sku_params` lead days, `sys_params` defaults, tier from `sku_planning_policy`, spike hits.

**(2) Ignored inputs that already exist.**
- **Open supply is completely absent.** `computeInventoryAlerts` calls only `getOnHandBySku` (`inventory-alerts.ts:124-128`). `core/supply.getOpenSupplyLines` — PO/WO/legacy_fg with `expectDate` — is never consulted, so a SKU with a PO landing in 3 days still raises the same `out_of_stock` alert as one with nothing on the way. The replenish page already does this netting (`replenish/service.ts:455-470`); the alert page does not. Two pages, two truths, and the alert is the one that pages people.
- **Learned lead time is ignored.** `alertDays()` reads only `sku_params.normal_lead_days / purchase_lead_days / logistics_lead_days` (`alert-threshold.ts:50-67`). `rollup_supplier_lead.leadP50Days / leadP90Days / onTimeRate` are materialized nightly by `src/jobs/rollup.ts:95-106` and **read by nobody** — only `leadStdevDays` has a consumer (`replenish/service.ts:483`). The distribution exists; the threshold uses the hand-typed archive number.
- **Batch expiry is ignored.** `pickPrimaryAlert` is called with only three facts (`inventory-alerts.ts:146`): `outOfStock`, `spike`, `lowStock`. `near_expiry` and `overstock` are declared in `ALERT_KINDS` (`alert-priority.ts:10`) and labelled in `ALERT_KIND_LABELS` but **can never be produced** — dead branches. `batch_stocks` + `replenish/expiry.ts` + `rules/risk-action.ts` already compute exactly those facts.
- **Plan events ignored.** `ops_plan_events` (promo/launch/delist) reach the replenish rows as tags (`replenish/service.ts` via `loadOpenPlanEventsBySku`) but never touch alert thresholds or suppression — a SKU deliberately delisted keeps alerting forever.

**(3) Open loop.** No outcome is ever recorded. `upsertAlerts` closes an alert after `autoCloseAfterDays` of non-hit (`alerts/engine.ts:74-82`) with `autoResolved=true`, and that is the entire epilogue. Nobody asks: *did the stockout actually occur?* (`stock_ledger` would answer), *was a PO/BH raised in response?* (`audit_logs action='draft_bh'` would answer), *was the alert a false positive because the cover number was wrong?* `system_alerts` has no `resolution_reason`, no `outcome`, no `false_positive`, and system writes deliberately skip `audit_logs` (`engine.ts:14-15`) — so **there is no alert history at all** beyond the current row state. Learning is not merely unimplemented; the data to learn from is being discarded.

**(4) Missing "why".** The payload is actually good here — `alertBasis` and `usedDefault` are rendered (`src/app/(app)/inventory/alerts/alerts-client.tsx:62`). What's missing: **why this daily rate** (`primaryDailySource` is a bare enum, no "external beat internal because net30=X over 30 days"), **why this priority rank** (`priorityScore` is an unexplained decimal string — `alert-priority.ts:58-65` computes `dailyAvg × (alertDays − coverDays)` with no `formula`/`terms` field), and **why this tier** (`tierSource` is `"policy"|"computed"` but not which period, which cut points, or the SKU's rank).

### A2. Sales spike

**Files**: `src/server/rules/sales-spike.ts`, `src/server/modules/report/sales-spike.ts`

**(1) Decision & inputs.** N consecutive days ≥ baseline × (1+rise%), baseline = prior 7-day average, min base qty guard. Input: Tmall JDY daily paid units only.

**(2) Ignored inputs.** The module's own limitation line says it: `"大促日历未接"` (`report/sales-spike.ts:181`). `ops_plan_events` with `kind='promo'` and `expectedUpliftPct` is a maintained table (`planning/plan-events.ts:19-41`) sitting right there. A promo you scheduled yourself fires a "spike" alert to `["ops","pmc","purchasing"]` (`jobs/system-alert-notify.ts:38`) — this is the textbook alert-fatigue generator. Also ignored: PDD/VIP series (noted), and `sku_planning_policy.xyz` — a Z-class SKU legitimately swings ±50% daily and should need a wider band than an X-class one; the threshold is global.

**(3) Open loop.** A spike alert never gets confronted with what happened next. `sales_monthly` and the daily series would let you ask "did demand sustain for 14 days, or was it a one-day promo blip?" — i.e. was this alert *worth* sending. Nothing measures it.

**(4) Missing "why".** `SpikeHit` carries `days`, `baseline`, `threshold` — good. But `SpikeResult.reason` (`rules/sales-spike.ts:118-120`), the single best explanation string in the module, is **dropped on the floor** in `report/sales-spike.ts:160-162`: only `days/baseline/threshold/risePct` are copied to the hit. And `gaps` (missing days counted as zero, which inflates the rise%) is computed and discarded too — a spike built on 4 gap days is far weaker evidence and the UI can't tell.

### A3. Alert engine, notification & todo projection

**Files**: `src/server/modules/alerts/engine.ts`, `src/jobs/system-alert-notify.ts`, `src/jobs/transfer-cost-watchdog.ts`, `src/server/rules/task-triggers.ts`, `src/server/modules/todo/*`, `src/jobs/todo-sync.ts`

**(1) Decision.** Idempotent upsert by `(category, dedupeKey)`, hysteresis auto-close, ack without status change; then category→role lookup, severity→priority, fixed due-day offsets.

**(2) Ignored inputs.**
- `rules/task-triggers.ts:80-85` maps severity→priority via a static table, and `todo/service.ts:534` sets due days from a static `{high:3, normal:7, low:14}`. Neither consults `priorityScore` (already computed and already stored in `paramsSnapshot` for cover alerts, `jobs/alert-watchdogs.ts:23`), nor `orderByDate` from `timePhasedNetReq` — which is the *actual* deadline. A stockout whose latest order date is tomorrow gets the same 3-day due date as one 40 days out.
- `ALERT_OWNER_ROLE` (`task-triggers.ts:49-59`) is a static category→role map. `work_items` history — who actually completes `inventory_cover` items on time — is never used to route.
- Ack history is never fed back: `upsertAlerts` refreshes a re-hit alert (`engine.ts:56-59`) **without clearing `ackedAt`**, so an alert acknowledged in June and re-triggered in September silently counts as already-handled.

**(3) Open loop — two of them.**
- `closeStaleProjectedItems` (`todo/service.ts:571-596`) cancels a todo when its source alert closes. Combined with `todo/stats.ts:132` (`cancelled` excluded from the denominator), **the fastest way to a perfect completion rate is to do nothing until the watchdog auto-closes the alert**. The stat cannot distinguish "person fixed it" from "condition evaporated". `autoResolved` is on the alert row and would settle it; `todo/stats.ts` never joins to it.
- `SUSPICIOUS_CLOSE_MINUTES` (`service.ts:33`) flags <10-minute closes but explicitly doesn't act on them, and nothing correlates a todo completion with any downstream document.

**(4) Missing "why" / architectural split.** `system_alerts` carries `sourceRule` and `paramsSnapshot` — but `alerts-client.tsx` renders neither (only title/detail/ack, `alerts-client.tsx:9-57`). The explanation is persisted and then hidden. Worse, `jobs/transfer-cost-watchdog.ts:65-113` **bypasses `upsertAlerts` entirely**, hand-rolling its own idempotency and inserting rows with no `ownerRole`, no `actionHref`, no `dedupeKey`, no `sourceRule`, no `paramsSnapshot` — so `transfer_cost` alerts fall to `alertOwnerRole()`'s `?? "admin"` default (`task-triggers.ts:88`) despite `ALERT_OWNER_ROLE` mapping them to `warehouse`. Two alert-writing code paths with different guarantees.

### A4. Replenishment suggestions

**Files**: `src/server/modules/replenish/service.ts`, `rules/{netreq,safety-stock,timephased,fusion,forecast,backtest}.ts`

**(1) Decision & inputs.** This is the most mature area: time-phased daily netting to first breach of safety stock, safety stock = z·σ_LT with demand *and* lead-time variance, MOQ/multiple, ref-gap suppression with `heldQty` preserved, Holt forecast gated by rolling backtest FVA, full `planExplain` chain and `decisionEvidence`.

**(2) Ignored inputs.**
- **Supplier OTIF / lead-time P50 never influence anything.** `rollup_supplier_lead.leadP50Days/leadP90Days/onTimeRate` are unread (see A1). `report/supplier-scorecard.ts` and `report/purchase-order-metrics.ts` compute per-supplier OTIF, and `po_promise_revisions` records promise churn — none of it discounts an in-transit PO's `expectDate`. A PO from a supplier with 40% OTIF is treated in `timePhasedNetReq.arrivals` as certain to land on `expectDate`.
- **Expiry ignored in supply.** `arrivals` and `onHand` are pure quantities; `batch_stocks.expiryDate` never nets out stock that will expire before it can be sold. `replenish/expiry.ts` already computes this per SKU.
- **`ops_plan_events.expectedUpliftPct` is display-only** (`planEventTags`), by explicit doctrine (D55). Defensible — but it means a known 3× promo produces zero demand signal, and there's no "scenario" affordance on the row either (`replenish/scenarios.ts` exists separately).
- **`ops_demand_submissions` never reaches the suggestion.** `replenish/reconcile.ts` compares ops submissions against Holt/naive baselines and flags |diff| ≥ 30% — a genuinely useful disagreement signal that the replenish row doesn't carry.

**(3) Open loop.** `report/closed-loop.ts` is the one real closed loop in the codebase (suggestion → `audit_logs draft_bh` → BH status → WO → SH receipt, with `adoptRate` and `deliveredRate`). Its gaps: (a) it measures *acceptance*, never *accuracy* — no comparison of `decisionEvidence.netRequiredQty` against what was actually ordered, or against realized demand over the horizon; (b) **rejection is invisible** — a suggestion the planner looked at and declined leaves no trace at all, since only drafted BHs are audited; (c) `heldQty`/`suppressReason` outcomes are unmeasured — nobody knows whether the ref-gap suppression (which the code's own comment says withheld 115,391 units) was right.

**(4) Missing "why".** Mostly present and exemplary (`planExplain`, `safetyMethod`+`reason`, `suppressReason`, `forecastTrusted`). Missing: **why this target cover** — `effectiveTarget` is a bare number with no note of which ABC class or which scoped-param layer (sku > brand > segment > global, `core/scoped-params.ts`) produced it; and **why no suggestion** — when `triggered` is false the row gets one explain line (`service.ts:~620`) but `suggestQty: null` reaches the UI with no machine-readable `noSuggestReason`.

### A6. Goals, S&OP, workbench
