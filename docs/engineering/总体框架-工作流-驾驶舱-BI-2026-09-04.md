# 供应链控制塔总体框架（2026-09-04）

来源：五份完备性审计（docs/engineering/完备性审计-*-2026-09-04.md）+ 生产库计数 + 总监需求 + D50–D68。可分享页：会话 artifact「供应链控制塔总体框架」。


## 01Diagnosis what the sweeps agreed on

- **Computed, sent, never shown** — The replenishment engine sends first-shortage date, latest order date, window-missed and safety quantity to the browser; the page renders none of them and cannot sort by them. The purchase-order list receives expected and confirmed dates and drops them. The transfer suggester computes lanes the client never reads. The supplier portal writes line-level promises that the internal PO view never selects.
- **Suggestions with no handoff** — Transfer suggestions cannot become a transfer document. A frozen S&OP version has no execution channel — freezing closes the only path from suggestion to request, so execution happens by hand outside the plan. Demand submissions are compared and then go nowhere. Saved what-if scenarios have no reader. Declined suggestions leave an audit row and are re-suggested next run.
- **Loops that measure and stop** — Closed-loop reporting computes adoption, delivery, accuracy and suppression review — and no path leads from any of it back to a parameter. Learned lead times are shown and never applied. Promotions have a table, an API, two consumers, and no page to write one.
- **One truth, several tellings** — Network on-hand differs between the position page and the warehouses page (one filters inactive warehouses, one does not) — and the cockpit shows both. Turnover means two things on two pages. Expired quantity appears as three different numbers. Three lead-time calibres live in three menu groups with no cross-links. Four pages compete to be the home screen.
- **The backfill choke point** — Lead-time entry is one row at a time with no export, no template, no bulk apply — 4,856 clicks to finish. Scoped parameters have a complete tested server and zero UI. Three live parameters (including a loss rate that flows into money) have no UI at all. The one backfill page that does batch, priority and progress well is the identity-claim card — buried in a decision-studio tab that the master-data menu does not point to.
- **Quality is an island** — The quality module is complete and self-consistent — and referenced by nothing else. Cases do not freeze batches, do not block release, do not feed supplier scores, do not raise alerts. Quarantine bins exist and no flow calls them. QC failure is an enum value with no downstream action.

## 02Framework five layers, one loop

- **观察 Observe**：What it is: 简道云 (34 contracts, 4.4M rows), RPA warehouse snapshots, JST and 用友 once authorized. Rule (D55): observation may inform and alert; it never posts, never drives a quantity, never claims an identity automatically. Status: the discipline holds end to end — tests prove the threshold cannot move from observed data. Gap: four 简道云 contracts, eight 用友 and three JST streams are synced and read by nothing, burning quota; the readiness page cannot tell you which.
- **事实 Truth**：What it is: the append-only ledger, latest snapshot per warehouse, batch stock by counting period, master data, parameters. Rule: one authority per fact — core/stock-view, core/valuation, core/velocity, rules/abc, core/supply; no local re-implementation. Status: the authorities are correct and well-commented. Gap: eight consumers still re-implement them (inventory analytics, snapshot balances, SKU panorama, expiry list, workbench, batch trace…), which is where the "three expired quantities" come from; and the master data itself is 10% populated.
- **决策 Decide**：What it is: pure rules with unit tests, read models cached with versioned keys, the four-screen cockpit, the workbench. Rule: every figure carries its calibre, its source, its as-of, and its explanation; a changed calibre bumps the key. Status: replenishment, alerting, segmentation and safety stock are genuinely strong. Gap: decision fields not rendered; three inputs the engine ignores though they exist (promotions, expiry, external velocity in the formula); cache bindings that omit the parameters they read.
- **行动 Act**：What it is: documents (BH/WO/PO/DB/PD/JS…), todos projected from alerts, notifications. Rule (R13): a human approves before any document exists; the system drafts, never posts on its own. Status: the document chain BH→WO→PO→SH→JS is complete and high quality. Gap: the handoffs into it — transfer suggestion → DB, frozen plan → execution, QC fail → return/case, quality case → quarantine — are missing; and short-close buttons exist as routes but not as UI.
- **学习 Learn**：What it is: the alert event ledger, stockout verification, precision by rule, suggestion accuracy distributions, suppression review. Rule (D61/D68): evidence, not scores; distributions, never a single accuracy number; nothing auto-tunes. Status: shipped this week and accumulating from today. Gap: the arrow back to Truth does not exist — no path proposes a parameter change from outcomes for a human to approve.

## 03Concepts the ideas that follow from the framework

- **1 · Readiness before intelligence** — A new planner logging in today sees "you have 56 stockout alerts". What they should see first is "your alerts are not yet trustworthy — fix these three things". Make readiness a first-class object: per capability (direct replenishment, external velocity, valuation, precision tuning), what data it needs, current coverage, what unlocks when it crosses the threshold. The workbench gets three readiness cards; the cockpit's header already carries coverage — promote it. The pilot page's "why zero direct" breakdown is the prototype.
- **2 · The backfill center** — One place to clear the four debts (lead times, costs, identities, snapshots) using the three things the identity card already does right: a queue sorted by value, system-proposed candidates with one-click confirm, and batch submission with a preview and a cap. Progress is shown as coverage against the pilot's blocked count, so every batch visibly unblocks candidates. This is the highest-leverage build in the system because everything downstream is throttled by it.
- **3 · One decision table: move first, then buy** — Transfer suggestions and replenishment suggestions are computed separately and shown on separate pages, and their cover-day figures are deliberately non-comparable. The director's decision is a single question per SKU: can a transfer cover it before an order must be placed? Put both in one table, ordered by latest order date, with the transfer path, its lane cost, and the residual to buy — then one action drafts the DB and one drafts the BH.
- **4 · Two hub objects: SKU 360 and Supplier 360** — Seven pages drill into SKU 360 and it is the only report with no source, no as-of, and no links out. The supplier page is pure CRUD while five read models hold the supplier's OTIF, lead-time calibres, quality history, price variance and payment terms. Make these two objects the places where every calibre about one thing sits side by side with its own header — never merged, always adjacent.
- **5 · Two homes, not four** — The cockpit is the organisation's exception-first view; the workbench is the person's actionable view. The morning digest becomes a workbench mode; the analytics dashboard keeps its menu name and drops the "驾驶舱" title. One landing per role; the redline chips and readiness cards decide what a director sees first.
- **6 · Alert → todo → outcome as the universal spine** — Every category now writes through one engine with an owner role, an action link, a why-list and an event ledger. Extend the spine to the four missing categories the data already supports — supplier licence expiry, promise breaches, OTIF collapse, quality-case overdue — and every workflow inherits acknowledgement, close-with-reason, todo projection, deadline, verification and precision for free.
- **7 · Close the learn → truth arrow, with a human in it** — After two to four weeks of alert events, precision by rule is real. The system should propose — never apply — parameter changes: "buffer days for tier B from 5 to 7 would have removed 14 false positives and added 1 miss; approve?" The scoped-parameter server, the audit trail and the approval inbox all exist; only the proposal object and its page are missing.
- **8 · Quality joins the chain** — QC failure drafts a return or a quality case; a case can freeze a batch through the existing quarantine bins; a case counts in the supplier score and raises an alert; batch trace and case scope share one recall range. The module is complete — it needs four arrows, not a rewrite.
- **9 · Explain the plan's own lifecycle** — The tier "freeze" runs daily and silently re-tiers; freezing an S&OP cycle removes the only execution path; declining a suggestion changes nothing next run. Each of these needs a stated lifecycle: freeze once per period with change audit; a frozen version gets an execution channel; a decline suppresses the same suggestion for a window with a reason.

## 06Implementation plan sequenced by dependency and value


### Wave 1 · connect what exists（running now · 3 branches · ~35 items · no business input）
- Planning: decision columns and sorting, confirm-modal fix, frozen banner and open-order column, over-buy warning wired, tier freeze lifecycle, NPD audit inside the transaction.
- Inventory: count-period scoping in six readers, count-date posting, on-hand authority for three re-implementations, alert landing pages, transfer suggestion → DB draft, lanes rendered.
- Procurement & reports: PO promises and revision timeline, list columns, short-close buttons, paused suppliers blocked, Supplier 360 drawer, lead-time learning into the scorecard, ⌘K search, price-compare gating, zero-KPI strips, registry mismatches, funnel/WIP links, SKU 360 provenance.
- Data foundation: bulk lead-time entry, export/import loop, scoped parameter UI, missing parameter UIs, defaults authority + guard, alert binding + guard, identity entry points, workbench readiness cards, run-now, channel write path, contract consumer column.
- Gate: full suite, production build check, one deploy in a quiet window (heavy builds during business hours restart the public tunnel).

### Wave 2 · close the handoffs（next · ~2 weeks · engineering only）
- Promotion calendar page; expiry enters replenishment; one latest-order-date calibre; execution channel for frozen versions; S&OP notifications; decline suppresses for a window.
- Move-first-then-buy decision table (transfer + replenish in one view, ordered by latest order date).
- Period lock for month close; ledger batch/balance/links/value; void and short-close for stock documents; async export kinds registered.
- OTIF on original promise; price-list maintenance; QC failure → return/case; four new alert categories through the engine; quality cases freeze batches via quarantine bins and feed supplier scores.
- Fold the duplicates: digest → workbench mode, report/npd → /npd tab, readiness tab → link; rename the analytics dashboard title.
- Post-deploy cache warm step in the deploy script (host-run, with production credentials handled by the script, not by hand).

### Wave 3 · learn → truth, with a human in the loop（after 2–4 weeks of alert events · needs business inputs listed below）
- Parameter proposals from precision and accuracy: proposal object, evidence view, approval through the existing inbox, applied via scoped parameters with audit. Nothing auto-applies.
- Learned lead time switches from observe-only to applied for suppliers with enough samples — once real purchase orders exist in the system.
- Supplier decision aid at the WO step (price, learned P90, observed P50, OTIF, score); sales definition reconciled and the consistency metric made comparable; identity coverage pushed toward 70% by the queue owner.
- Goals linked to the alerts and todos that move them; forecast-to-period-end.

### Wave 4 · structural moves（once the daily habit exists）
- One director home (merge the two remaining first screens by role); phone layout pass; personalization and "what changed since my last visit".
- In-transit warehouse and transfer write-off end to end (bonded and cloud warehouses); reconcile the two batch truths and open the batch-posting gate with the readiness card that already exists.
- S&OP close-out with plan-vs-actual variance and the pilot A/B read-out; value-weighted tiering repointed after a comparison period.
- Procure-to-pay's last two arrows: settlement → payables due schedule → cash view; traffic leading indicator once identity coverage allows.

## 07Acceptance how we will know it worked

- **Readiness index**：capabilities unlocked ÷ capabilities defined（now: 3 of 8 (alerts, cockpit, segmentation) → target: 6 of 8 by end of wave 2）
- **Lead-time coverage**：SKUs with parameters ÷ active finished goods（now: 520 / 5,376 · 167 candidates blocked → target: 100% of S/A/B finished goods; 0 blocked on this reason）
- **Identity coverage**：mapped platform SKUs ÷ platform SKUs（now: 23.8% → target: ≥ 70%）
- **Handoff completeness**：suggestion types with a one-click draft path（now: 1 of 3 (replenish → BH) → target: 3 of 3 (+ transfer → DB, frozen plan → BH)）
- **Single-authority violations**：local re-implementations of shared facts（now: 8 known → target: 0, guarded by a test）
- **Alert precision by rule**：true ÷ scored, from the verification job（now: accumulating since today → target: ≥ 70% for stockout alerts within 6 weeks）
- **Transaction adoption**：documents created in the system per month（now: 7 stock docs, 3 POs all year → target: every physical PO and transfer has a document）
- **Homes**：pages competing to be the first screen（now: 4 → target: 2 (cockpit, workbench)）

## 08What only the business can provide the unlocks

- **Lead-time parameters**：Wave 1 makes entry fast and shows progress; someone still has to enter them. Unblocks 167 pilot candidates and everything downstream of direct replenishment.
- **Purchase orders in the system**：Three all year. Every procurement metric, learned lead time, OTIF and promise tracking is empty until real orders flow through BH→WO→PO.
- **Daily RPA snapshots**：One snapshot day exists. Month-end history, snapshot-warehouse turnover and the position page's history all wait on this delivery.
- **The definition of "sales"**：Internal month table is 1.6× platform paid units. Until one is chosen, the consistency metric and the inventory ratio denominator are not comparable.
- **An identity queue owner**：Exact matches wait for a human click by design. Wave 1 puts the queue where the owner will find it; someone has to own it.
- **Costs and a price source**：One cost row; the margin report has read "price source pending" since v1. Valuation covers 99.98% via the finance observation, but value-weighted tiering and margin need the real thing.
- **At least one department goal, a promotion calendar entry, a demand submission**：Three tables at zero rows. The features exist; they show nothing until used.
- **JST and 用友 authorization; the Feishu app**：Code and probes are ready. Direct-message reminders and two data sources wait on console clicks.
- **Decisions D50–D68 confirmed**：Thresholds, ratio target, tier cut points, ownership rule — all set under delegated authority; parameters can change later, but the defaults deserve a look.

## 09Guardrails how this is executed without breaking things
